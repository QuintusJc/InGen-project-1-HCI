“Animating Empathy” — Motion Storyboard

Readable, predictable, emotionally safe gestures across six scenes.

Greeting

Timing: turn 30°, pause 0.5s, small wave.

Principles: Anticipation, low amplitude for safety.

Acknowledgment

Timing: slow nod with smooth ease to mid, then back.

Principles: Overlap (head lags torso), weight.

Offer Help

Timing: ~2.5s total. Elbow → wrist → fingers.

Principles: Arcs, follow-through finger settle.

Reassure

Timing: Lean slightly, palms up, remain open.

Principles: Staging to chest & hands; clarity.

Wait Mode

Timing: chest rises 1–2 cm every ~2.5s.

Principles: Subtle cycle to avoid “frozen” feel.

Farewell

Timing: arm trails torso by ~0.3s, then settles.

Principles: Follow-through, overlapping action.

Timeline Flow

  1. Anticipate (pause, orient)
  2. Move (curved arc, ease-in/out)
  3. Rest (settle, tiny overlap)
